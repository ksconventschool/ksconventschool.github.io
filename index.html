School Code of Conduct

Discipline, respect and responsibility help create a positive learning environment.

Discipline & Responsibility

  • Maintain punctuality and regular attendance.
  • Follow the instructions of teachers and school staff.
  • Keep classrooms and school premises clean.
  • Use school property responsibly.

Respect & Good Manners

  • Speak politely and respectfully with everyone.
  • Treat classmates with kindness and dignity.
  • Maintain appropriate behaviour inside and outside classrooms.
  • Promote honesty, cooperation and good citizenship.
